This is a comprehensive reference textbook on the current state of knowledge concerning the free boundary curve shortening flow and its ancient solutions in the case of a compact free boundary.

Chapter 1 serves as a basic introduction to the free boundary curve shortening flow, and presents the $$n = 1$$ case of Stahl's long time existence arguments. 

Chapter 2 presents the behaviour of the flow at the extinction time, with a particular focus on recent results on stability. 

Chapter 3 is about the classification of the convex ancient solutions to the free boundary curve shortening flow in compact and convex domains.
