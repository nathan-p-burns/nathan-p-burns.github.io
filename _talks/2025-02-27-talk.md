### Classification of Ancient Solutions to the Free Boundary Curve Shortening Flow in Convex Domains

A talk on the classification of convex ancient solutions to the free boundary curve shortening flow in convex and compact domains. This talk was a slight revision to the one given in Punta Del Este.
