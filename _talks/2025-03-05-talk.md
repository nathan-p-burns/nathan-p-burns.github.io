### Classification of Ancient Solutions to the Free Boundary Curve Shortening Flow in Convex Domains

A talk on the classification of convex ancient solutions to the free boundary curve shortening flow in convex and compact domains. This talk was a slight revision to the one given in Punta Del Este and the MATRIX Institute. 

Slides to the talk may be found [here](https://nathan-p-burns.github.io/files/convex-ancient-fbcsf1-slides.pdf).
